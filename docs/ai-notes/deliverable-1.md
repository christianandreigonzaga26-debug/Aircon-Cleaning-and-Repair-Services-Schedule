# AI Usage Note - Deliverable 1

For this deliverable, I utilized an AI assistant (Gemini) to help format and structure my planning documents to meet the lab requirements. 

* **Scoping and Backlog:** I provided my initial CRUD concept and table. I prompted the AI to help me translate those actions into the formal Agile "Actor, Action, Benefit" format. The AI also helped brainstorm standard Acceptance Criteria for these specific routes.
* **Wireframing:** I prompted the AI for layout advice. It provided low-fidelity, text-based diagrams of standard scheduling interfaces, which I screenshotted and adapted to serve as my required wireframes. 
* **Tool used:** Google Gemini.
